HamloRevolution 0.1.0 - rhythmic multi-effect, VST3, Windows x64 (HamloProd)

Controls
  MIX          Blend of the effected and the dry signal.
  RATE         Step length in FREE mode (ms).
  STEPS        Pattern length.
  SWING        Delays every second step.
  HUMAN        Small random timing per step.
  FADE         Crossfade at the step edges (no clicks).
  TIME MIX     How much of the TIME lane is heard.
  TIME DEPTH   Strength of half-time, reverse, freeze, tape stop, double.
  GLITCH MIX   How much of the GLITCH lane is heard.
  GLITCH DEPTH Strength of repeats, stutters, gaps, scratches.
  MOTION MIX   How much of the MOTION lane is heard.
  MOTION DEPTH How far each MOTION step moves the target.
  SMOOTH       Glide between MOTION steps.
  ORDER        Which lane processes first (T = time, G = glitch, M = motion).
  CHARACTER    How GENERATE writes patterns: MINIMAL, MUSICAL, EXPERIMENTAL, DESTRUCTION.
  GRID         Step length as a note value.
  TRIPLET      Triplet steps.
  SYNC / FREE  Steps locked to the tempo or free in ms.
  TIME         Turns the TIME lane on or off.
  GLITCH       Turns the GLITCH lane on or off.
  MOTION       Turns the MOTION lane on or off.
  TARGET       What MOTION moves: volume, low pass, high pass, width, crush.
  STRETCH      TAPE bends pitch; RHYTHM keeps the pitch; CLEAN plain.
  LISTEN       GENERATE follows the hits it heard in the input.
  GENERATE     New pattern for every unlocked lane.
  GEN (lane)   New pattern for this lane only.
  LOCK (lane)  Keeps this lane as it is when GENERATE is pressed.
  ON           the lamp switch; off = the dry signal

Presets: the screen top left - the arrows step through the presets (or the mouse wheel; click the name for a menu).
The same presets are in the DAW's program list.

Mono and stereo. The latency is fixed and reported to the DAW.

Uninstall: Settings -> Apps -> HamloRevolution, or Start menu -> HamloProd -> Uninstall HamloRevolution.
